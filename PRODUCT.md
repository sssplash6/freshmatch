# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High school seniors in Uzbekistan and CIS countries assessing their academic profile for Western university applications. They open this with a mix of anxiety and hope — they want an honest read of their chances, not encouragement. They are not applying yet; they are trying to understand whether applying is realistic.

## Product Purpose

A two-section quiz that evaluates a student's academic background and interests, then returns a fit label and a curated list of recommended universities. Success is a clear, credible answer delivered in under five minutes — no fluff, no hedging.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The voice of a good counselor: frank, unhurried, and free of hype. The interface earns trust through restraint, not reassurance.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS (Notion, Linear) — no productivity-tool gray, no sidebar nav
- College marketing sites — no stock photos, no hero banners, no aspirational copy
- Typeform / quiz-builder aesthetic — must not look like an off-the-shelf quiz tool
- Edu-tech (Duolingo, Khan Academy) — no gamification, no bright colors, no hand-holding

## Design Principles

1. **Honest over optimistic.** The interface reflects the product's purpose: frank assessment, not a sales pitch. No softening language, no celebration UI.
2. **Precision signals care.** Tight typography, consistent spacing, and zero decoration earn trust. Sloppiness reads as unreliability.
3. **The content is the design.** No chrome, no illustration, no background texture. The questions and answers are all that matters.
4. **Calm through whitespace.** Generous negative space reduces the anxiety of a high-stakes interaction. Nothing crowds the user.
5. **One thing at a time.** The quiz is sequential by design. The interface reinforces focus and never shows competing elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No color-only state indicators (always pair with position or weight).
